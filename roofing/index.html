Insurance Claims

We Handle the Paperwork. You Just Get Your Roof Fixed.

Most homeowners don’t know what their policy covers until they’re standing under a water stain. Insurance claims for roof damage can drag on, and adjusters don’t always catch everything on the first visit. We document damage thoroughly, put together a scope your insurer can’t easily dispute, and stay in contact with your adjuster through the process.

We’ve done this enough times to know what gets approved and what gets missed.

  • We photograph and document all damage before starting any work
  • We’ll meet your adjuster on-site if you want us there
  • We only recommend what needs to be replaced — not everything
  • Supplement requests handled if the initial payout is short

What We Do

Straight Roofing Work, No Upselling

We’re a small crew based in Enid. We take on residential jobs we can do well, not every job that comes in. If something’s beyond what we handle, we’ll tell you straight.

01

Storm Damage Inspection

We climb up and look at everything — shingles, flashing, ridge cap, gutters, soffit, fascia. You get a written report of what we found. Free, no strings.

02

Full Roof Replacement

Tear-off and replacement with architectural shingles, metal, or whatever the project calls for. We pull the permit, handle disposal, and clean up after ourselves.

03

Hail & Wind Repair

Spot repairs for isolated damage that doesn’t require a full replacement. If your roof has good life left in it and localized damage, we’ll fix what needs fixing and leave the rest.

04

Emergency Tarping

Storm rolled through last night and now you have a hole. We tarp it fast to stop the water from spreading into your attic and walls while the full repair gets scheduled.

05

Insurance Claim Assistance

We write scopes, document damage, and communicate with your adjuster. You don’t need to understand the insurance side of it — that’s on us.

06

Gutters & Fascia

Gutters that pull away, bent fascia board, or soffit damage that’s letting critters in. We handle these as part of a roofing job or as a standalone repair.

How It Works

Four Steps From Damage to Done

We keep the process simple and keep you in the loop at every stage. Most jobs from first call to finished roof take one to two weeks, depending on material availability and weather.

Request a Free Inspection

No payment, no contract required for an inspection. We charge for work, not for looking.

1

Free Inspection

We come out, climb up, and assess the damage ourselves. You get a written report of what we found — whether it’s a $200 repair or a full replacement situation.

2

We Document & File

If insurance is involved, we put together the documentation your adjuster needs. Photos, measurements, scope of damage. We’ve done this enough times to know what they’re looking for.

3

Work Gets Scheduled

Once your claim is approved or you’ve decided to pay out of pocket, we lock in a date. We order materials and get it on the calendar — usually within a week or two.

4

Roof Goes On

The crew shows up on time, does the work, and cleans up every nail and shingle scrap before leaving. We walk the job with you before we call it done.
