Storm Damage & Insurance

Hail Came Through.
Here’s What to Do.

Oklahoma hail seasons are real. If your neighborhood caught a storm, your roof may be damaged even if it looks fine from the street. The damage that ends up costing money is usually what you can’t see from the ground.

We inspect free of charge, document what we find, and work directly with your insurance adjuster if you have a claim open.

  • Lifted or missing shingles along the ridgeline
  • Hail impact marks on vents, flashing, and gutters
  • Granule buildup in the gutters after a storm—sign of shingle breakdown
  • Dark spots or water staining on attic decking
  • Daylight visible from inside the attic
